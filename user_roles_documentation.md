# Dynamic Real Estate Marketing - User Roles & System Architecture

This document breaks down the structure of the Dynamic Real Estate Marketing application, detailing the hierarchy, folder structure, and individual capabilities of the four primary user roles: **Sales Agent**, **Supervisor**, **Manager**, and **Admin**.

The application is built using React and utilizes Firebase (Authentication & Firestore) for managing access and data. Below is the module breakdown based on the system's structure.

---

## 1. Sales Agent Module (`src/Sales/Components`)
The **Sales Agent** is the frontline employee responsible for interfacing with potential clients, collecting prospect data, and managing their daily field/routine work.

### Folder Structure
* `Login.jsx` & `RegisterUser.jsx`: Entry points for agent authentication and onboarding.
* `dashboard.jsx`: The primary layout and navigation shell for the sales agent.
* `addprospect.jsx`: The form interface to submit new prospect leads into the system.
* `viewprosp.jsx`: A data table view where the agent can review and track the prospects they have personally added.
* `officeandsite.jsx`: Form and log view dedicated to tracking prospective buyers' physical visits to the main office or the property sites.
* `Report.jsx`: A local analytics/report view where agents can measure their own performance and keep track of their numbers.

### Role Capabilities
* **Data Entry**: Can add new leads and prospects.
* **Visit Logging**: Track client appointments (both office walk-ins and site tours).
* **Self-Tracking**: Can view only their own generated prospects and visits, preventing them from accessing leads generated by other agents to ensure data privacy.

---

## 2. Supervisor Module (`src/Supervisor`)
The **Supervisor** oversees a specific team of Sales Agents. Their module is designed around team tracking, data aggregation, and agent onboarding.

### Folder Structure
* `dashboard.jsx` & `menu.jsx`: The navigation layout providing higher-level access to the supervisor.
* `registerSalesAgent.jsx` & `sales.jsx`: Tools for onboarding new sales agents and assigning them to the supervisor's team.
* `addprospect.jsx` & `viewprop.jsx`: Supervisors can also act in a sales capacity. They have the ability to submit their own prospects and view aggregated prospects for the agents assigned under them.
* `officeandsite.jsx`: Allows the supervisor to monitor office and site visits conducted by their team.

### Role Capabilities
* **Team Management**: Register new sales agents and monitor their activities.
* **Aggregated View**: Can view prospect tables, log entries, and site visits for all agents that report directly to them.
* **Direct Sales**: Can still function as an agent if necessary, adding and tracking their own prospects.

---

## 3. Manager Module (`src/Manager`)
The **Manager** operates at an executive level, overseeing multiple Supervisors and all their underlying Sales Agents. Their focus is heavily analytical and performance-oriented.

### Folder Structure
* `dashboard.jsx`, `menu.jsx`, & `index.js`: The central managerial overview, featuring high-level charts and global metrics.
* `managemnt.jsx`: Interface for managing the broader team hierarchy (both Supervisors and Sales Agents).
* `performance.jsx`: Detailed analytics and KPI tracking for both individual agents and entire supervisor teams.
* `Report.jsx`, `sales.jsx`, & `officeandSite.jsx`: Aggregated reporting tools to view everything happening under the manager's purview.
* `managerform.jsx`: The manager's interface for direct prospect logging if necessary.

### Role Capabilities
* **Performance Tracking**: View comprehensive charts outlining sales data, prospect volumes, and visit statistics filtering by dates or entire teams.
* **High-Level Overview**: Instantly view the sum total of prospects generated, deals closed by the sales department, and cross-team metrics.
* **Broad Access**: Can view data entered by *any* supervisor or sales agent beneath them.

---

## 4. Admin Module (`src/Admin`)
The **Administrator** has unrestricted, system-level access to everything in the application. They manage the global state, handle system configuration, and oversee all interactions.

### Folder Structure
* `LoginAdmin.jsx` & `auth.jsx`: A separate, highly secure authentication portal tailored specifically for admins.
* `dashboard.jsx` & `adminMenu.jsx`: The global layout and control panel where all data across the entire organization is visible.
* `adminprospect.jsx` & `adminsales.jsx`: Global data views giving admins the ability to edit, delete, or oversee any prospect or sale in the database—regardless of who submitted it.
* `adminsitemgmt.jsx`: Configuration panel where the admin can add, remove, or modify the physical "Sites"/Properties that the company is currently marketing.
* `adminvisits.jsx`: Unrestricted view of all office and site visits logged company-wide.
* `followup.jsx`: A tracking panel to ensure prospects are being routinely followed up on and that deals aren't stagnating.

### Role Capabilities
* **System Configuration**: Manage dropdown items, property locations, and global settings.
* **Unrestricted Access**: Ability to see, edit, or delete any record in the system across all agents, supervisors, and managers.
* **Troubleshooting & Overrides**: Handle issues, correct data entry errors, and manage user access roles (assigning who is a manager, supervisor, etc.).

---

## Summary of the Data Flow
1. **Sales Agents** generate raw data (Prospects, Visits, Sales) from the field.
2. **Supervisors** monitor that data for their specific cluster of agents, making localized team decisions.
3. **Managers** analyze that data across *multiple* teams to track overall company performance and generate strategic reports using charts.
4. **Admins** manage the infrastructure, ensuring the system runs smoothly and has the right configuration parameters (like Site names) available for the rest of the staff to use.
